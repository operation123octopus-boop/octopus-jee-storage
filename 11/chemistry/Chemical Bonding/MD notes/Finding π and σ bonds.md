# How to find σ and π bonds


## Cheatsheet: Spotting Sigma and Pi Bonds
### The Golden Rules

* Every single connection between two atoms contains exactly one sigma bond.
* Any extra bonds layered on top of that first connection are pi bonds.

| Bond Type | Bond Components |
|---|---|
| Single (A-B) | 1 sigma bond |
| Double (A=B) | 1 sigma bond + 1 pi bond |
| Triple (A≡B) | 1 sigma bond + 2 pi bonds |

---
## The Instant No-Drawing Shortcuts
### 1. The Sigma Math Trick
For open-chain molecules, skip drawing entirely:

* Formula: Total sigma bonds = (Total number of atoms) - 1
* Ring Exception: If the molecule forms a closed ring (like benzene), the total sigma bonds simply equal the total number of atoms.

### 2. The Pi Blueprint
Since pi bonds only exist in multiple bonds, look exclusively at the structural bottlenecks:

* Count 1 pi bond for every double bond present.
* Count 2 pi bonds for every triple bond present.

---
## ⚠️ The Danger Zone: Hidden Bonds in Condensed Formulas
When text formulas hide the bonds, use this cheat list to instantly spot them without drawing Lewis dots:

| Condensed Group | Real Structure | Sigma inside group | Pi inside group |
|---|---|---|---|
| -CH3 (Alkyl) | 3 single C-H bonds | 3 sigma | 0 pi |
| -CN (Cyano) | C≡N triple bond | 1 sigma | 2 pi |
| -CHO (Aldehyde) | C=O double bond + C-H | 2 sigma | 1 pi |
| -COOH (Acid) | C=O double bond + C-O + O-H | 3 sigma | 1 pi |
| -COO- (Ester) | C=O double bond + C-O | 2 sigma | 1 pi |

---
## Quick Diagnostic Examples## Propene (CH3CH=CH2)

   1. Total Atoms: 3 Carbon + 6 Hydrogen = 9 atoms.
   2. Sigma Calculation: 9 - 1 = 8 sigma bonds.
   3. Pi Calculation: One visible double bond = 1 pi bond.

## Acetic Acid (CH3COOH)

   1. Total Atoms: 2 Carbon + 4 Hydrogen + 2 Oxygen = 8 atoms.
   2. Sigma Calculation: 8 - 1 = 7 sigma bonds.
   3. Pi Calculation: Contains the -COOH group trap = 1 pi bond.
