# Bohr's Model: Core Formulas & Relations

### Constant Quantities
* **Bohr Radius ($a_0$):** $\approx 0.529 \text{ \AA} = 5.29 \times 10^{-11} \text{ m}$
* **Ground State Energy of H ($E_1$):** $-13.6 \text{ eV} \approx -2.18 \times 10^{-18} \text{ J}$
* **Rydberg Constant ($R_\infty$):** $\approx 1.097 \times 10^7 \text{ m}^{-1}$

---

## 1. Orbit Radius ($r_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$r_n = \frac{4\pi \varepsilon_0 n^2 \hbar^2}{m e^2 Z} = \frac{\varepsilon_0 h^2 n^2}{\pi m e^2 Z}$$
* **Simplified Form:**
  $$r_n = a_0 \frac{n^2}{Z} \approx 0.529 \times \frac{n^2}{Z} \text{ \AA}$$
* **Proportionality:** 
  $$r_n \propto \frac{n^2}{Z}$$

## 2. Orbital Velocity ($v_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$v_n = \frac{e^2 Z}{4\pi \varepsilon_0 n \hbar} = \frac{e^2 Z}{2 \varepsilon_0 h n}$$
* **Simplified Form:**
  $$v_n = v_1 \frac{Z}{n} \approx \left(2.18 \times 10^6 \right) \times \frac{Z}{n} \text{ m/s}$$
* **Fine Structure Constant Relation ($c$ = speed of light):**
  $$v_n = \left(\frac{c}{137}\right) \times \frac{Z}{n}$$
* **Proportionality:**
  $$v_n \propto \frac{Z}{n}$$

## 3. Total Orbital Energy ($E_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$E_n = -\frac{m e^4 Z^2}{8 \varepsilon_0^2 h^2 n^2}$$
* **Energy Component Relations:**
  $$\text{Kinetic Energy (K.E.)} = -E_n = \frac{1}{2} |U_n|$$
  $$\text{Potential Energy (P.E., } U_n) = 2E_n = -2 \text{ K.E.}$$

### Value in Electron-Volts (eV)
* **Formula:**
  $$E_n = -13.6 \times \frac{Z^2}{n^2} \text{ eV}$$

### Value in Joules (J)
* **Formula:**
  $$E_n = -2.18 \times 10^{-18} \times \frac{Z^2}{n^2} \text{ J}$$
* **Proportionality:**
  $$E_n \propto \frac{Z^2}{n^2}$$

## 4. Time Period ($T_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$T_n = \frac{2\pi r_n}{v_n} = \frac{4 \varepsilon_0^2 h^3 n^3}{m e^4 Z^2}$$
* **Simplified Form:**
  $$T_n = T_1 \frac{n^3}{Z^2} \approx \left(1.52 \times 10^{-16}\right) \times \frac{n^3}{Z^2} \text{ s}$$
* **Proportionality:**
  $$T_n \propto \frac{n^3}{Z^2}$$

## 5. Orbital Frequency ($f_n$ or $\nu_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$f_n = \frac{1}{T_n} = \frac{m e^4 Z^2}{4 \varepsilon_0^2 h^3 n^3}$$
* **Simplified Form:**
  $$f_n = f_1 \frac{Z^2}{n^3} \approx \left(6.58 \times 10^{15}\right) \times \frac{Z^2}{n^3} \text{ Hz}$$
* **Proportionality:**
  $$f_n \propto \frac{Z^2}{n^3}$$

## 6. Centripetal Acceleration ($a_n$)
* **Exact Formula:**
  $$a_n = \frac{v_n^2}{r_n} = \frac{\pi m^2 e^8 Z^4}{4 \varepsilon_0^3 h^4 n^5}$$
* **Proportionality:**
  $$a_n \propto \frac{Z^3}{n^4}$$
