# How to solve *Block over Block* problems

Always remember that:

$F_{appliedmax} = f_{frictionmax}$

<font color="yellow"> Because the applied force must balance the max friction force, otherwise the body will fall apart </font>

You can then **form equations**.

Some prerequesties:
1. Find  $f_{frictionmax}$ by focussing on the *upper block*
2. Find $a _{common}$ of the total system of blocks (**this will be a equation generally**).
3. Then find the *pseudo force* for the *upper block*.
4. Use the above equation.
