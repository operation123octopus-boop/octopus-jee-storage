# Question thought process: 

1. Spot that there is a quadratic that **cannot** be **factorised** using *Sridharacharya* or *Middle-term splitting*. 
2. Then use reduction of higher degree quadratic by **substituting $\alpha$ or $\beta$**.

    The steps here are (***note that this can vary***):
    - Isolate the $\alpha^2$.
    - Then *Squaring both sides*.
    - Put the value of $\alpha^2$ in the new *RHS*.
    - Then simplify and you will get a *linear relation*.
